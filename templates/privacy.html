Scripts and reading preferences

$name stores your scripts, cards, reading preferences, and unfinished rehearsal positions locally on your device. There is no account to create, and this release does not sync scripts between devices.

Manual reading works offline. Word highlighting runs at your selected pace without sending your script to a timing service.

Microphone and speech

Manual reading does not require microphone permission or a speech-model download. Voice following and recording are not available in this release.

Information about retained speech software and its licenses is available in the app’s Privacy and Acknowledgements screens.

Diagnostics and Apple services
