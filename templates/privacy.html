Scripts and reading preferences

$name stores your scripts, cards, reading preferences, and unfinished rehearsal positions locally on your device. There is no account to create, and this release does not sync scripts between devices.

Manual reading works offline. Word highlighting runs at your selected pace without sending your script to a timing service.

Microphone and speech

Manual reading does not require microphone permission or a speech-model download. On versions with Voice Following or recording, Speech Deck requests microphone access when you choose those features. Recording also requests camera access. Recordings stay on your device until you choose to save or share them.

Information about retained speech software and its licenses is available in the app’s Privacy and Acknowledgements screens.

Diagnostics and Apple services

Technical diagnostics can contain script identifiers and short excerpts of script text. Review device logs before choosing to share them with support.

Apple may provide aggregate or opt-in App Store and TestFlight measurements, diagnostics, and feedback according to your settings and Apple’s policies.

Optional app analytics and crash reports

In versions that offer these settings, sharing is off until you choose to enable it. You can change either choice in Settings → Privacy at any time. Your choice does not affect access to the app or paid features.

Usage analytics help us improve onboarding, the reader, recording, and Pro. When enabled, Speech Deck sends feature events and basic app and device details to PostHog in the EU. An installation identifier lets us count repeat use. A purchase event can include the plan you selected.

Crash reporting is a separate choice. When enabled, Speech Deck can send crash information such as the app version, operating system, and stack frames to PostHog in the EU. Stack frames show where the app stopped. We use this information to diagnose crashes.

These reports do not include scripts, script titles, transcripts, audio, video, payment receipts, or transaction IDs. We use the information to improve Speech Deck. We do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Turning sharing off stops future reporting for that option and discards queued reports on your device. It does not remove reports that PostHog already received. Contact us at the address above to ask about retention or request deletion. See PostHog’s privacy policy.
