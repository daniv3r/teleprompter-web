A few things to know.

Does it follow my voice?
This first release uses the reading pace you choose. Voice following and recording are not included.
Do scripts sync between devices?
Scripts stay on each device. Sync and import/export are not included. Keep a separate copy of important scripts.
Is it free?
The first release is free and requires no account. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases in this release.
How do I get started?
Write a script, open the reader, and press Play when you are ready. Visit Support for a short walkthrough.
